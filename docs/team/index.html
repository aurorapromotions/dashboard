Team & Access

Add people, choose how they sign in, and decide which tools they can use.

AdminFull access to every tool, and can manage the team.
Team leadA member with Full access to one or more tools, for example a sales lead.
MemberLimited access: sees only their own records, can add and edit, not delete.

Turning someone off stops them signing in right away. Their records stay. A person's name is also their sales rep name in Sales Navigator.
