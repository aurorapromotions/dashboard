Order Acceptance

Terms & Conditions that clients accept with each order and invoice.

Coming next: send an order for acceptance

From an invoice in Sales Navigator, the rep will send the client a private link (from their own Gmail, after their approval) showing the order summary, these Terms & Conditions and the Stripe/Square invoice. The client ticks "I accept" and types their name; the dashboard records who accepted, when, and which version of the terms. Payment marks the invoice as paid.

Until then, you can share the terms with clients using Print / Save as PDF on the Terms & Conditions tab.
